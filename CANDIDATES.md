# Candidates — Monday Morning Punchlines

Gate: one joke about Monday mornings, 1–200 characters (Python `len`, UTF-8).
All character counts below were verified with code, not counted by hand.

Judge criteria, in priority order (from competition.yaml / RULES.txt):
1. Really about Monday mornings.
2. Earns a real laugh — a turn or punchline that lands, not just a complaint.
3. Surprises — the twist is not the first thing anyone would think of.
4. Economy — few words, every word doing work, no wasted setup.
5. Fresh — not a rerun of tired Monday clichés.
